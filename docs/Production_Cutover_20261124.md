## Storage and fault recovery

An NVS receipt stores the transition phase, start-marker sequence and local baseline date with an integrity checksum. Once intent is durable, ordinary count writes, recovery and seasonal mutations pause. The current directory moves to `/CC/2026-TEST` or `/GC/2026-TEST`. The archive includes earlier reports, logs and SD checkpoints. Flash-only test records are verified there before cutover completes. Source and destination both present during the rename phase is a conflict. The original pending-events NVS namespace is preserved.

The new active directory retains the usual `/CC/2026` or `/GC/2026` path. A committed kind-7 flash record starts at zero with a higher sequence than its predecessor. The receipt becomes Ready only after readback of the marker archive and the new SD count checkpoint. The old flash banks stay intact. Archive replay and bank reclamation verify each record against the directory on its side of the session boundary. A reboot restores the latest production counts and pending aggregate. A pre-cutover card fails the production marker check. The flash recorder can continue with a validated clock and saved HA settings during an SD or MQTT outage.

The ordinary journal CSV exporter excludes `-TEST` directories. Use `--tests` to export their records explicitly. Production journal records supply the historical occupancy reports and are protected from the file manager's retired-record cleanup.
